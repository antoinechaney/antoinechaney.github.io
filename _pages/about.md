I am a PhD student in Development Economics at DIAL (*Développement, Institutions et Mondialisation*), a research unit of LEDa, Université Paris Dauphine - PSL under the supervision of Najat El Mekkaoui and Kenneth Houngbedji.

My research lies at the intersection of **development economics**, **environmental economics**, and **energy economics**, with a focus on the MENA region.
