I am a PhD student in Development Economics at DIAL (*Développement, Institutions et Mondialisation*), a research unit of LEDa, Université Paris Dauphine – PSL. I am jointly supervised by Najat El Mekkaoui and Kenneth Houngbedji.

My research lies at the intersection of **development economics**, **environmental economics**, and **energy economics**, with a focus on the MENA region.

Feel free to reach out. I'm always happy to discuss research, collaborations, or ideas.
